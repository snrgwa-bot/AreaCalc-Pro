Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Summary: Your projects, measurements and quotes stay on your device. We never receive them. The app does send anonymous usage statistics so we can see which features matter and catch crashes, and you can switch that off in Settings › Privacy. There are no ads, no trackers and no account to create.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how AreaCalc Pro Sq FT ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Do Not Collect

AreaCalc Pro Sq FT is designed with privacy in mind. We never collect, store or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, postal address)
  • Background or continuous location tracking
  • Your IP address. Our servers do not store it or use it.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV)
  • Receipts, transaction identifiers or the prices you paid
  • Project data you enter: project names, job addresses, client contacts, measurements or quotes
  • Any other personal or sensitive information

The one thing the app does send is anonymous usage statistics, described in the next section. You can turn them off.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

To improve the app, AreaCalc Pro Sq FT sends anonymous usage information: a random installation identifier that is not linked to your identity or Apple Account; your device model and iOS version; the app version and build; your device's time zone, region and language; accessibility settings in use, such as VoiceOver or Larger Text; when sessions start and end and the last screen viewed; which features you use, for example a project created, a section measured, a project duplicated, pinned or deleted, an export completed, or a change of unit or theme; paywall views and taps; purchase, trial and restore events, meaning the plan and its status only; App Store review prompts; and crash and performance diagnostics provided by Apple through MetricKit.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, postal address, location, IP address, advertising identifier, vendor identifier, receipts, transaction identifiers, prices paid, or anything you enter in the app, including project names, job addresses, client contacts, measurements and quotes.

  • Not linked to you, not used for tracking: the installation identifier is random, exists only in this app, and is deleted when you delete the app. We never combine it with data from other apps or companies.
  • Not shared or sold: we do not share this data with third parties and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Where it is stored: on a database operated for us by our hosting provider, Supabase, accessible only to us.
  • How long: usage records are deleted automatically after 13 months, and crash diagnostics after 6 months.
  • Turn it off: Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data. When it is off, nothing is collected or sent, and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device.
  • Delete what was sent: copy your Installation ID from Settings › Privacy and email it to us. We will delete every record linked to it.

Data Storage

All data you create within the app, including:

  • Project names
  • Section measurements and shapes
  • Pricing information
  • App settings and preferences

is stored locally on your device only. We never receive it, and it is never uploaded to our servers. The only information that leaves your device for us is the anonymous usage statistics described above, and only while that setting is on. Measuring, saving and exporting all work with no internet connection.

Third-Party Services

AreaCalc Pro Sq FT contains no advertising, no advertising SDK and no cross-app tracking. It uses our own analytics, not a third-party analytics service.

  • Supabase hosts our anonymous usage statistics on our behalf, as described above. It does not use the data for its own purposes.

Three further features reach the network, and all three talk only to Apple:

  • In-app purchases. Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.
  • Filling in a job address. Described under Location below.
  • The update check. On launch, at most once a day, the app asks Apple's public App Store lookup service whether a newer version exists. The request contains only the app's identifier — nothing about you or your projects.

Location

If you turn on Job Details and tap the address button, the app asks iOS for your current location once, converts it into a street address, and writes that address into the project on your device.

  • Location is requested only when you tap that button — never in the background, and never continuously.
  • The coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to be turned into an address. That request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • The resulting address is stored in your project on your device. It is not sent to us, and we never receive your coordinates.
  • You can decline the permission, or revoke it later in iOS Settings, and every other feature keeps working.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent data to anyone. The anonymous usage statistics stay with us and with Supabase, who store them for us. We may disclose information if the law requires it. When you use the export or share feature, your takeoff goes directly from your device to the destination you choose, such as email or messages, and never passes through our servers.

Data Security

Your project data is protected by your device's built-in security features. We recommend:

  • Using a device passcode or biometric lock
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Exporting important projects to maintain backups

Children's Privacy

AreaCalc Pro Sq FT does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The anonymous usage statistics contain no personal information and are never used to identify anyone. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Your Rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is no personal profile to access or correct. You remain in control of everything the app does hold:

  • Turn off usage statistics in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data.
  • Delete usage statistics already sent by emailing us the Installation ID shown in Settings › Privacy.
  • Delete your project data in the app at any time, or by deleting the app, which removes everything stored on your device.
